# Bombas cosmetic ricochet - CC0 audio

Original: [aust_paul, bullet ricochet.wav, Freesound #30932](https://freesound.org/people/aust_paul/sounds/30932/), published 2007-02-14, **Creative Commons 0 (CC0)**. Original recording includes four ricochet variations in one mono WAV, offered as the public high-quality MP3 preview at https://cdn.freesound.org/previews/30/30932_15696-hq.mp3.

Retained source: `audio_sources/30932_aust_paul_ricochet_cc0.mp3`. Four derived 22,050 Hz mono signed 16-bit PCM WAV samples in `assets/ricochet_{1..4}.wav` are generated by `python3 tools/bombas/build_ricochet_assets.py`, which extracts the four short source impulses, attenuates low frequencies and applies a short fade. These are played in 3D from a local cosmetic metal impact, not from a real bouncing bullet.
