# Bombas positional bullet whizz audio - CC0 sources

- Bullet Fly By, GO_s0unds123, https://freesound.org/people/GO_s0unds123/sounds/730351/ , 2024-04-02, Creative Commons Zero (CC0). Source: public HQ MP3 preview at `https://cdn.freesound.org/previews/730/730351_15855556-hq.mp3`.
- Bullet Crack.wav, morganpurkis, https://freesound.org/people/morganpurkis/sounds/386854/ , 2017-04-01, Creative Commons Zero (CC0). Source: public HQ MP3 preview at `https://cdn.freesound.org/previews/386/386854_5937039-hq.mp3`.

Both pages explicitly mark the recordings CC0, including commercial use and derivative redistribution. The original MP3 previews are retained in `audio_sources/`, and this project's mono, 22050 Hz, 16-bit PCM 0.6-second variations in `assets/bullet_{near,far}_{1..6}.wav` are generated by `python3 tools/bombas/build_whizz_assets.py`. They use filtered flyby excerpts plus optional quieter supersonic crack, equalized fades and peak normalization. The resulting sound files are loaded by the client locally, spatialized by the game engine at the closest point of a real bullet's world-space path, and packaged in the universal client PK3.
